# Community guidelines

Be respectful, constructive, and specific. Welcome contributors with different backgrounds
and levels of experience. Critique ideas and evidence rather than people. Credit others'
work and respect privacy.

Harassment, discriminatory abuse, threats, sexual harassment, and publishing someone's
private information without permission are not acceptable in project spaces.

The maintainer may edit or remove inappropriate content and restrict participation for
violations. Enforcement should reflect the severity and context of the behavior. For
non-sensitive concerns, contact the maintainer through a project issue. Do not post
private details publicly; a dedicated private reporting channel is not yet configured.
